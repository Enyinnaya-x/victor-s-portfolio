about

How I think about the work

I care about architecture — how a system is put together, why it holds up under load, and what breaks first when it doesn't. That's a more interesting problem to me than any single framework.

I've spent the most time recently in Node.js and TypeScript, but I came up through Spring Boot and Java, and Laravel and PHP, and those still shape how I model data, write migrations, and think about concurrency. PostgreSQL and MySQL both show up depending on what the problem needs.

I'm not looking to sound impressive. I'm trying to get better at reasoning about state, correctness, and failure — and building things that are still readable a year later.
